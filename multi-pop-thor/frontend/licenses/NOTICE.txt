Patched ROCKNIX EmulationStation frontend

Original frontend source: ROCKNIX/emulationstation, revision cada856d86e3115fbbf0bce09dd761b0ee8fa9fd.
The local modifications adjust the B navigation popup and savestate manager placement on the Thor 4400 x 1080 canvas. The Multi Pop game carousel also pages three games left with L1 and three games right with R1 on that canvas.

These files retain the original notices for the frontend, statically embedded pugixml, NanoSVG, id3v2lib, rcheevos/libretro-common, cpp-httplib, ZIP/MD5 utility code, and frontend font resources. Embedded-source-notices.txt retains source-specific notices, including zlib, FLAC, CHD, LZMA/7-Zip, and their contributors.

RapidJSON and system-header notices accompany the compiler/runtime notices. Graphics, media, and other shared libraries are supplied by the installed ROCKNIX system rather than bundled in this package.
